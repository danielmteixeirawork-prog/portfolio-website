About me

I studied Electrical and Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior de Engenharia do Porto (ISEP), then started my career programming PLCs and HMI/SCADA systems for wastewater treatment plants at KLINGER Portugal. Since November 2024 I've been at Maxiglobal, where I moved from Systems Engineer into a Product Manager role while continuing to build software.

My work sits at the intersection of industrial automation, IT infrastructure, and full-stack development — integrating equipment like UPSs, generators, PLCs, and IP cameras into monitoring platforms, and building the software (C#/.NET, React, TypeScript, SQL) that makes that data reliably available.

.c embedded

agv-line-follower

Automated Guided Vehicle that follows a black line on a white surface, with firmware written in C for an ATmega328P microcontroller. Three line-detection sensors are read continuously; the firmware decides whether the vehicle is centred, drifting left or drifting right, and corrects course by driving the two DC motors independently (differential steering), all in a tight sense-decide-actuate loop.

  • C
  • ATmega328P
  • Embedded
  • Motor control
.sch hardware

temperature-signal-conditioning

Analog front end for a resistive temperature sensor. The circuit turns the sensor's change in resistance into a voltage and scales it to the input range of an A/D converter, so the full measurement range is captured with good resolution. A microcontroller reads the digitised value, converts it to temperature, and uses it to monitor the device and keep it within its target range.

  • Analog design
  • Signal conditioning
  • ADC
  • Microcontrollers
