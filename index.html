About me

I studied Electrical and Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior de Engenharia do Porto (ISEP), then started my career programming PLCs and HMI/SCADA systems for wastewater treatment plants at KLINGER Portugal. Since November 2024 I've been at Maxiglobal, where I moved from Systems Engineer into a Product Manager role while continuing to build software.

My work sits at the intersection of industrial automation, IT infrastructure, and full-stack development — integrating equipment like UPSs, generators, PLCs, and IP cameras into monitoring platforms, and building the software (C#/.NET, React, TypeScript, SQL) that makes that data reliably available.
